# MONETRA — App Store Optimization (ASO) & SEO Strategy
**Target Stores:** Apple App Store (iOS) & Google Play Store (Android)  
**Primary Markets:** United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Germany  

---

## 1. App Store Metadata (iOS)

- **App Name:** `Monetra: Visual Budget Planner` (30 characters)
- **Subtitle:** `See your money differently` (27 characters)
- **Promotional Text:** `Instant financial clarity in under 5 seconds. Calculate your Safe-to-Spend daily allowance, track budgets with visual rings, and reach your savings goals.`
- **Primary Category:** Finance
- **Secondary Category:** Productivity
- **Target Keywords (100 characters max):**  
  `budget,expense tracker,money manager,spending tracker,daily budget,savings goal,personal finance,cashflow`

---

## 2. Google Play Store Metadata (Android)

- **App Title:** `Monetra: Visual Budget & Money` (30 characters)
- **Short Description (80 characters):**  
  `Visual-first personal finance. Safe-to-spend daily limits, budgets & savings.` (77 chars)
- **Full Description:**
```markdown
Stop fighting with complicated spreadsheets and confusing accounting dashboards. 

Meet Monetra — the visual-first personal finance app designed to help you understand, control, and grow your money in less than 5 seconds.

WHY MONETRA?
Traditional finance apps show what you spent yesterday. Monetra answers the single question that matters most every morning:
"How much can I safely spend today?"

KEY FEATURES:

✨ SAFE TO SPEND TODAY
Our deterministic algorithm calculates your exact daily discretionary allowance based on your remaining monthly budget, upcoming bills, and savings commitments. Spend with total confidence.

📊 VISUAL-FIRST DASHBOARD
Understand your financial health at a glance with interactive category donuts, progress rings, and cash flow trends. No complex tables. Just pure clarity.

⚡ 3-SECOND QUICK LOG
Logging a coffee or lunch takes 3 quick taps. Fast, smooth, and effortless.

🎯 SAVINGS GOALS & MILESTONES
Track your emergency fund, dream vacation, or down payment with motivating progress rings and milestone projections.

📅 SUBSCRIPTION & BILL CALENDAR
Uncover recurring costs. Track monthly and annual burn rates so you never get surprised by an auto-renewal again.

🔒 100% PRIVATE & LOCAL-FIRST
Your financial records belong to you. Monetra operates completely offline with an encrypted local SQLite database. No bank account passwords or balances are ever shared with third-party servers.

🌍 MULTI-CURRENCY SUPPORT
Seamlessly manage your finances in USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), CAD (CA$), AUD (AU$), CHF, AED, and MAD.

Download Monetra today and see your money differently.
```
