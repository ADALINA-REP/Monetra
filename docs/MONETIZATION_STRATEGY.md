# MONETRA — Mobile Monetization & Growth Strategy
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Business Model:** Premium Freemium (Value-Aligned Utility)  

---

## 1. Monetization Philosophy

Finance apps frequently alienate their initial users by gating basic manual tracking behind aggressive paywalls. Monetra’s philosophy is:
> *"The Free Tier must be genuinely delightful and fully functional forever. The Premium Tier should provide high-leverage foresight, deep historical analytics, and automated convenience."*
